# Transparent XGEN Intel Score

The XGEN Intel Score is not a verdict. It is a transparent summary of observable characteristics.

M1 establishes the scoring engine interface and a conservative baseline score.

## M1 Method

- Start at 50.
- Add no hidden adjustments.
- Add no authority or concentration adjustments until the relevant milestones are implemented and tested.
- Return every adjustment in the response, including an empty list when no adjustments apply.

## Planned V1 Adjustments

Potential future adjustments must be explicit and documented before implementation.

Examples:

- Mint authority revoked: positive characteristic.
- Mint authority active: risk characteristic.
- Freeze authority active: risk characteristic.
- High top-token-account concentration: risk characteristic.
- High owner concentration, once owner resolution is reliable: risk characteristic.

No adjustment may claim that a token is definitively safe or malicious.
